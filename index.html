📚 About

I am an M.Sc. student at South China Normal University. My research focuses on LLM agents, reinforcement learning, and graph learning.

My goal is to build reliable LLM agents that can solve long-horizon, complex, and collaborative tasks by learning from feedback, improving through reinforcement learning, and coordinating with other agents.

If you would like to discuss any details about my papers or research, please feel free to email me.

🔍 Research Interests

  • LLM Agents: long-horizon task solving, tool-based reasoning, planning, and collaboration.
  • Reinforcement Learning: interaction-based training, long-horizon credit assignment, and stable agent improvement.
  • Graph Learning: text-attributed graphs, structured communication, and faithful inference.
