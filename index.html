📚 About

I am an M.Sc. student at South China Normal University. My research focuses on LLM agents, reinforcement learning, and graph learning.

My goal is to build capable LLM agents for long-horizon, complex, and collaborative tasks.

If you would like to discuss any details about my papers or research, please feel free to email me.

🔬 Research Overview

My research focuses on building capable agents for complex and collaborative tasks—studying how agents communicate, reason faithfully with structured evidence, and improve through long-horizon interaction.
